Transfer Learning in Computer Vision

Transfer learning reuses knowledge from a model trained on one dataset or task and adapts it to another. In computer vision, this often means starting from a network pretrained on a large dataset such as ImageNet and fine-tuning it for a smaller target problem.

Benefits:
1. reduces training time
2. improves performance when labelled data is scarce
3. provides strong initial visual features
4. makes practical deployment easier in domain-specific settings

Typical workflow:
- load pretrained weights
- replace or adapt the classifier head
- fine-tune some or all layers
- evaluate on the target dataset

Transfer learning is especially useful in medical imaging, industrial inspection, and niche classification tasks where collecting large labelled datasets is difficult. It is also an important bridge between foundational vision models and real-world applications.
