Vision Transformers

Vision transformers apply transformer-style architectures to image data. Instead of relying only on convolutions, the image is divided into patches and processed as a sequence with attention mechanisms.

Core ideas:
1. image patches become token-like inputs
2. self-attention models long-range visual relationships
3. positional encodings preserve spatial information
4. large-scale pretraining often improves performance significantly

Why vision transformers matter:
They show that attention-based architectures can work beyond language and compete strongly with convolutional networks in vision tasks.

Applications include:
- image classification
- detection and segmentation
- multimodal image-text models
- vision-language assistants

Vision transformers are especially relevant for representation learning because they highlight a broader trend in AI: shared architectural ideas can often transfer across modalities such as text, images, and even video.
