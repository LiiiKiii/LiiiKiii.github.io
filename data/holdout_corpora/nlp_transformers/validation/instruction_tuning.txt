Instruction Tuning and Alignment

Instruction tuning adapts a pretrained language model so it can better follow user requests expressed in natural language. Instead of learning only from raw text continuation, the model is optimized on prompt-response pairs that resemble interactive use.

Goals of instruction tuning:
- improve helpfulness
- improve obedience to task instructions
- reduce irrelevant or confusing outputs
- make the model more suitable for dialogue and assistance

Related alignment ideas:
1. supervised fine-tuning
2. reinforcement learning from human feedback
3. preference optimization
4. safety filtering and refusal policies

Why it matters for AI-Pedia:
The project is not just about retrieving resources. It also involves presenting recommendations and optional summaries in a way that is useful to learners. Instruction-tuned models are therefore relevant to the explanatory layer, even if the retrieval and ranking pipeline remains primarily symbolic and heuristic.
