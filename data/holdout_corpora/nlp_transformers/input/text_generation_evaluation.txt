Evaluating Text Generation

Text generation quality is difficult to measure because multiple outputs may be acceptable, and quality depends on factors beyond literal overlap.

Important evaluation dimensions include:
- fluency
- factual accuracy
- relevance to the prompt
- coherence across longer responses
- usefulness for the user
- safety and harmlessness

Automatic metrics can help, but they are limited. Common examples include BLEU, ROUGE, perplexity, and newer embedding-based similarity metrics. Human evaluation is still important, especially when quality depends on explanation, trust, or educational value.

For systems like AI-Pedia, evaluation should not stop at whether a summary sounds fluent. It should also ask whether the retrieved resources are relevant, diverse, trustworthy, and genuinely helpful for learning.
