Semantic Segmentation

Semantic segmentation predicts a class label for every pixel in an image. Instead of drawing a single box around an object, the model produces a dense understanding of scene structure.

Why segmentation matters:
- self-driving systems need road and obstacle regions
- medical imaging needs precise anatomical boundaries
- satellite imagery needs land-use mapping
- robotics needs detailed scene understanding

Core concepts:
1. encoder-decoder architectures
2. upsampling and skip connections
3. pixel-wise supervision
4. overlap-based metrics such as IoU and Dice score

Representative models include U-Net, Fully Convolutional Networks, DeepLab, and transformer-based segmentation systems.

Semantic segmentation is important for representation learning because the task requires both local detail and global context. A useful visual model must capture edges, shapes, textures, and scene-level relationships at the same time.
