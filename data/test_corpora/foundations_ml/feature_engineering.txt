Feature Engineering and Data Representation

Feature engineering transforms raw data into representations that machine learning models can use effectively. Before deep learning became dominant, feature design was one of the most important parts of building an AI system.

Examples of features:
- word counts or TF-IDF vectors for text
- edge or texture descriptors for images
- statistical summaries for sensor data
- domain-specific ratios in finance or medicine

Why representation matters:
1. Better features often lead to better generalization
2. Simpler models can perform strongly with informative inputs
3. Poor features can hide useful structure from the model

Connections to modern AI:
Deep learning automates much of feature learning by discovering hidden representations directly from data. Even so, preprocessing and representation choices still matter, especially for tabular data, retrieval systems, and hybrid pipelines.

In AI-Pedia, representation quality matters at two levels: the learner documents must be represented well enough for keyword extraction, and the retrieved resources must be represented well enough for ranking and recommendation.
