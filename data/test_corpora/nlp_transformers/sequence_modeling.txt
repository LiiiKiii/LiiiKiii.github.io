Sequence Modeling in NLP

Sequence modeling studies how to represent and predict ordered data such as sentences, speech signals, or time series. In NLP, sequence models must capture syntax, semantics, and long-range context.

Earlier sequence architectures included:
- n-gram models
- recurrent neural networks
- LSTMs and GRUs

Modern sequence modeling is dominated by transformers, which use self-attention instead of recurrence. This change improved parallelism and made it easier to scale to longer contexts and larger datasets.

Key ideas:
1. autoregressive prediction
2. encoder-decoder learning
3. contextual representations
4. long-range dependency handling

Applications:
- language modeling
- translation
- summarization
- dialogue systems
- speech recognition

Sequence modeling remains a core conceptual bridge between classical NLP and contemporary large language models.
