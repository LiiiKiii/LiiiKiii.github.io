Natural Language Processing Techniques

NLP Overview
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.

Traditional NLP Techniques:
1. Tokenization: Breaking text into words or sentences
2. Stemming and Lemmatization: Reducing words to base forms
3. Stop Word Removal: Filtering common words
4. Bag of Words: Simple text representation
5. TF-IDF: Term frequency-inverse document frequency weighting
6. N-grams: Sequence of n consecutive words

Deep Learning for NLP:
- Word Embeddings: Word2Vec, GloVe, FastText
- Sequence Models: RNN, LSTM, GRU
- Attention Mechanisms: Self-attention, multi-head attention
- Transformers: BERT, GPT, T5, RoBERTa

NLP Tasks:
1. Text Classification: Spam detection, sentiment analysis
2. Named Entity Recognition: Identify entities (names, places)
3. Machine Translation: Translate between languages
4. Question Answering: Answer questions from text
5. Text Summarization: Generate concise summaries
6. Language Generation: Create coherent text
7. Sentiment Analysis: Determine emotional tone
8. Part-of-Speech Tagging: Identify grammatical roles

Modern Pre-trained Models:
- BERT: Bidirectional understanding
- GPT-3/GPT-4: Large language models
- XLNet: Permutation-based training
- ELECTRA: Efficient pre-training
